FLYBACK POWER SUPPLY BOM COST COMPARISON (2026 estimate)
============================================================

------------------------------------------------------------

------------------------------------------------------------

Flyback controller IC            : 0.80 – 1.50 €
MOSFET (or integrated switch)   : 0.30 – 1.20 €
Transformer                      : 1.00 – 3.00 €

Optocoupler / feedback          : 0.10 – 0.40 €
Passives (caps, resistors)      : 0.80 – 2.00 €

TOTAL BOM COST                  : 3.0 – 7.5 €

------------------------------------------------------------
2. MEDIUM / CONSUMER PSU (30–150 W)
IC class: Infineon ICE2 / ICE3, TI UCC256xx
------------------------------------------------------------

MOSFET (external)               : 0.80 – 2.50 €
Transformer                     : 2.50 – 6.00 €

Current sensing (shunt / Hall)  : 2 – 10 €
Isolation / digital isolators   : 2 – 8 €

============================================================
SUMMARY
============================================================

Small charger (IC-based)   : 3 – 7.5 €
Medium PSU                 : 6 – 17 €
Heavy PSU                  : 30 – 120 €
Your RP2350 design         : 12 – 40 €

============================================================
KEY INSIGHT
============================================================

IC-based systems:
- minimal BOM
- fixed control law
- hardware-optimized feedback loops

MCU-based system (yours):
- higher BOM
- fully programmable control
- replaces analog control loop with DSP + ISR
